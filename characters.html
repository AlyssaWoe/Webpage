Characters & Cast

Isolated Character Portraits Without Background

Discover the founding figures of the American experiment. Every character below is presented in full theatrical costume with background removed to highlight their stage presence.

ORIGINATED BY LIN-MANUEL MIRANDA

Alexander Hamilton

First Secretary of the Treasury • Founding Father

An ambitious, orphaned immigrant from the Caribbean who uses his relentless writing ability and intellect to rise to the center of American power. He serves as Washington’s aide-de-camp, writes the Federalist Papers, and builds the nation's financial system.

Signature: "My Shot" • "Non-Stop"
ORIGINATED BY LESLIE ODOM JR.

Aaron Burr

Third Vice President • Narrator & Rival

Hamilton's lifelong foil, friend, and eventual killer. Cautious, poised, and patient, Burr embodies the philosophy of "Talk less, smile more" and "Wait for it" until ambition compels him into the duel that ruins both men.

Signature: "Wait for It" • "The Room Where It Happens"
ORIGINATED BY PHILLIPA SOO

Eliza Schuyler

Hamilton's Wife • Philanthropist & Keeper of Legacy

The gentle, deeply moral second daughter of Philip Schuyler. Despite enduring betrayal and devastating grief over her son Philip's death, Eliza forgives Alexander and dedicates the final fifty years of her life to telling his story and raising funds for orphans.

Signature: "Helpless" • "Burn" • "Who Lives, Who Dies"
ORIGINATED BY RENÉE ELISE GOLDSBERRY

Angelica Schuyler

Eldest Schuyler Sister • Intellectual Peer

Brilliant, fiercely witty, and commanding, Angelica seeks "a mind at work" in revolutionary New York. Though instantly captivated by Alexander, she sacrifices her own romantic feelings for Eliza's sake, remaining Hamilton’s most trusted intellectual confidante.

Signature: "The Schuyler Sisters" • "Satisfied"
ORIGINATED BY CHRISTOPHER JACKSON

George Washington

General of the Army • First President

The towering father figure of the Revolution. Washington mentors Hamilton, guiding his fiery temper with grave wisdom about the burdens of leadership, the inevitability of historical judgment, and the peaceful transfer of constitutional power.

Signature: "Right Hand Man" • "One Last Time"
ORIGINATED BY DAVEED DIGGS

Thomas Jefferson

Secretary of State • Third President (Dual: Lafayette)

Flamboyant, silver-tongued, and fiercely protective of Southern states' rights. Returning from France in Act II, Jefferson locks horns with Hamilton in venomous cabinet rap battles, challenging federal taxation and national banking power.

Signature: "What'd I Miss?" • "Cabinet Battle #1"
ORIGINATED BY JONATHAN GROFF

King George III

Monarch of Great Britain • Royal Foil

The pompous British monarch who treats the American revolution like a spurned romantic breakup. Appearing in three show-stopping, darkly comedic British-invasion pop solos, he warns that governing without a king is vastly harder than winning a war.

Signature: "You'll Be Back" • "What Comes Next?"
ORIGINATED BY ANTHONY RAMOS

John Laurens

Abolitionist & Soldier (Dual: Philip Hamilton)

Hamilton's closest friend who dreams of leading the first all-Black battalion to free enslaved people. Tragically killed at the end of the war, his spirit is carried forward by Alexander's son Philip, who later dies in a duel defending his father's honor.

Signature: "The Story of Tonight" • "Blow Us All Away"
ORIGINATED BY JASMINE CEPHAS JONES

Peggy Schuyler

Youngest Schuyler Sister (Dual: Maria Reynolds)

In Act I, Peggy is the cautious, rule-following youngest sister ("And Peggy!"). In Act II, Jones doubles as Maria Reynolds, an alluring, vulnerable woman whose husband blackmails Hamilton, setting off the scandal that destroys Alexander's political career.

Signature: "The Schuyler Sisters" • "Say No to This"
ORIGINATED BY OKIERIETE ONAODOWAN

Hercules Mulligan

Tailor & Revolutionary Spy (Dual: James Madison)

An undercover spy posing as a tailor to British officers, passing crucial battle intelligence to Washington. In Act II, Onaodowan plays the chronically ill, razor-sharp James Madison, Jefferson’s steadfast co-conspirator and political strategist.

Signature: "Yorktown" • "Washington on Your Side"
